## Notes on interpretation

- “Canonical mutation path” means the command service/handler chain. Direct repository writes are otherwise only present in the two findings above (V1) and in reconcilers/restore infra, which are intentionally repository-level.
- “Persistence owner” lists the primary keys; reward/economy keys are additionally touched by every rewarding completion.
- Where a row says “Verified compliant”, conformance is limited to the operations exercised by the cited tests plus the traced call path. It does not claim device-level behavior.
- Findings that are cross-cutting (P1, P5, V4) are attached to the most relevant row and detailed in `architecture-violations.md`.
