# Why Pebble Exists (Pebble North Star Guide)

People don't fail because they don't know what to do.

They fail because productivity apps feel like work.

---

## The Human Core of Pebble

1.  **Companion Mindset**: Pebble should feel like taking care of a tiny companion. It has warmth, feeling, and a personality.
2.  **Emotional Reward**: Every completed action must feel emotionally rewarding. The physical drop of a pebble into the jar, the crow’s encouraging feedback, and physical haptics are central to this loops.
3.  **Visible Progress**: Progress should be visible and tactile, not buried in charts or tabular statistics.
4.  **No Administrative Overhead**: The app should reduce friction, not add organization complexity.

---

## The Feature Filter

Every future feature (AI assistants, Focus timers, Calendar integrations, Gamified milestones, Sanctuary) must justify itself against this question:

> **Does this help someone start doing, or does it just encourage them to organize more?**

If it does not directly accelerate execution or emotional reward, do not build it.
